**About OORT**

OORT: The Data Cloud for Decentralized AI

Founded in May 2021, OORT is pioneering the future of decentralized AI with an end-to-end solution that empowers both enterprises and individuals to collect, process, and monetize AI data. Built on the Olympus Protocol, OORT’s native Layer 1 blockchain, our decentralized AI data cloud is anchored by OORT DataHub, the flagship product for scalable and secure AI data collection and processing.

Backed by leading investors including Taisu Venture and Red Beard Ventures, and supported by strategic grants from Microsoft and Google, OORT has earned the trust of world-renowned partners and clients such as Binance Smart Chain, Tencent Cloud, Seagate, Dell, and Githon (formerly known as Lenovo Image).

Olympus Protocol

The Olympus Protocol is a proprietary blockchain technology of OORT that can be deployed by any party interested. The detailed technical description and performance are given in the [Yellow Paper](https://blazebyte1.standard.us-east-1.oortstorages.com/computecoin-consensus-and-security.pdf). Connecting all DePin devices globally across OORT, Olympus uses its specific proof of honesty (PoH) consensus algorithm that ensures each node works transparently and utilizes network resources optimally in a decentralized manner. [Learn more about the proof of honesty consensus algorithm](https://www.forbes.com/sites/digital-assets/2024/02/24/decentralized-ai-on-blockchain-rivals-openais-lead/?sh=176a64ff7c28).
